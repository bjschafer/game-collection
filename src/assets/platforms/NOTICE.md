# Platform artwork

These logos identify the systems represented in a personal game collection.
They are third-party brand artwork, not original artwork by this project.
Brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

## Sources

- 33 logos: **Dan Patrick's console logo set**, via the
  [PRO100BYTE/console-logos mirror](https://github.com/PRO100BYTE/console-logos),
  pinned to commit `1de47931607ddf83cbc982d776b68e6cc3864ad7`.
  The mirror's MIT license is preserved in `LICENSE.console-logos`; its README
  separately states that the logos belong to their respective rights holders.
- `amiibo.svg`: Nintendo's official logo, distributed by
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amiibo.svg).
- `switch2.svg`: Nintendo's logo, distributed by
  [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nintendo_Switch_2_logo_transparent_black.svg).
  Commons identifies both Nintendo files as `PD-textlogo`, with trademark notices.
- `generic.svg`: a neutral question mark created for this project, under the
  project's MIT license. It does not impersonate an unknown platform's logo.

`sources.json` records the exact download URL, credit, and SHA-256 for each
third-party file. Files are preserved as downloaded, including their colors
and proportions. The SVGs contain their own vector geometry and require no
external images or fonts. The generic question mark uses a system font.
